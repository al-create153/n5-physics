How answers are marked

Type your number and choose a unit. Your answer is right if it is rounded to the same number of significant figures as the least precise value in the question, or one fewer, or up to two more. You can use any sensible prefix, so 2.4 kJ and 2400 J are both fine. For large or small numbers type 3.0e8 or 3.0x10^8.
